## Illustrative interface values

Numbers used in interactive public reconstructions—for example a representative
solved day, price floor, or per-leg margin—must be labeled as illustrative or
representative. They demonstrate the mechanics of a decision system and are not
additional portfolio outcome claims.

## Verification checklist

Before publishing a changed claim:

- Confirm the population, time window, and comparison baseline.
- Distinguish observed results from modeled or directional estimates.
- Remove customer, member, operator, and contract identifiers.
- Keep phase-specific numbers attached to the correct system version.
- Update the page, this ledger, and any résumé copy together.
