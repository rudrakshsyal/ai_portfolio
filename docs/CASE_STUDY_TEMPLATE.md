# Case Study Template

Use this template for new flagship work. Do not force every project to fill
every section; remove sections that do not add evidence.

## 1. Ten-second view

- Project name
- One-sentence decision problem
- One principal outcome
- Compact system or result visualization

## 2. Context

- Who experienced the problem?
- What decision or workflow was failing?
- Why did the problem matter?
- What constraints shaped the solution?

## 3. Personal responsibility

- What did Rudraksh own?
- What was shared with engineering, product, operations, finance, or leadership?
- Which implementation and evaluation decisions were personally made?

## 4. System

- Inputs and source grain
- Processing or modeling stages
- Serving or decision path
- Integration boundary
- Human review or fallback

## 5. Technical decisions

For each important choice:

- decision;
- alternatives considered;
- reason for the selection;
- failure mode or tradeoff accepted.

## 6. Evaluation

- Offline metrics
- Online or shadow validation
- Test-set construction
- Baseline
- Monitoring
- What would cause the system to defer?

## 7. Product outcome

- Behavior or decision changed
- Adoption
- Time or cost saved
- Revenue, margin, quality, or customer impact
- Whether the outcome is direct, estimated, or directional

## 8. Interview probes

Keep these privately, outside the public repository:

- hardest debugging issue;
- architectural alternative;
- failure and recovery;
- disagreement or cross-functional tradeoff;
- what would be redesigned today.

## Publication checklist

- [ ] Material metrics appear in `CLAIMS_LEDGER.md`

- [ ] Visual has accessible text
